What does PurrSafe actually do?

It gives your cat a single private log — meals, weights, health notes, behaviour, vet visits — and lets you ask questions against it later instead of scrolling.

Is it available yet?

PurrSafe is in private beta. Join the waitlist and we’ll send your invite; when the store listing is live this page links straight to it.

Who can see my cat’s memories?

You. Entries are tied to your account and are not published or shared with other users.

Spec

Native <details>/<summary>. No JavaScript — keyboard operation, screen-reader expanded state and find-in-page all come free, and none of them survive a hand-rolled div accordion. Summary is 48px min-height, teal 700/17px, with the default marker removed and a + that rotates 45° when open. The open state adds a 1px --ps-border rule under the summary and squares its bottom corners so the panel reads as one surface. The rotation is a transition, so prefers-reduced-motion collapses it to an instant swap.
