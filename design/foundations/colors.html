Two rules that are not negotiable

1. Coral is never text and never a text background on white — #E29578 is 2.39:1 against white and against a white label sitting on it. It is legal as a fill behind --ps-ink (5.8:1), which is why the product-proof callout numerals are charcoal-on-coral.
2. #9B9490 never carries text. Use --ps-ink-muted — and note it is #636363, not the app's #6B6B6B, because #6B6B6B is only 4.4:1 on --ps-canvas-deep.
