FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What does PurrSafe actually do?

It gives your cat a single private log — meals, weights, health notes, behaviour, vet visits — and lets you ask questions against it later instead of scrolling.

Is it available yet?

PurrSafe is in private beta. Join the waitlist and we’ll send your invite; when the store listing is live this page links straight to it.

Who can see my cat’s memories?

You. Entries are tied to your account and are not published or shared with other users.

Can it tell me if a food is dangerous?

It can help you record and look up what your cat ate and flag what you have logged before. It is not a medical authority — anything urgent goes to your vet.

Does it work for more than one pet?

Yes. Each pet gets its own profile and its own archive, and the assistant only reads the one you select.
